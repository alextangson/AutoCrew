# Personal IP Video Loop

The user approves creative outcomes at four gates; the agent operates the authorized work between them. Continue through retrieval, QA and delivery, not merely job submission. This Skill describes orchestration; it does not install a background worker or webhook.

## Start or resume

Every video is AutoCrew-bound; the unbound "direct dispatch" mode (`~/Movies/AutoCrew直派`) was retired on 2026-09-29 because finished cuts never reached the board. Before touching footage or covers, resolve the content_id (title search, or `autocrew_content summary` on the candidate; `autocrew_video match` is closed once the AutoCrew library has the content ontology enabled). If none exists, stop and ask the Claude session to import the script — do not open a side folder. Then read [autocrew-workspace.md](references/autocrew-workspace.md); its service-bound paths, adopted production profile and state ownership replace the independent-folder/template instructions below. Never initialize or overwrite AutoCrew's generated workflow-state.json.

Report every artifact you produce with `autocrew_content record` (kind aroll / cut / srt / cover / chatcut_project; same request_id on retry) and follow the `next_action` in its receipt and the `next` field of `autocrew_content summary`. The work is finished when summary shows 待发布: that happens by itself once the founder approves the cut and picks the covers in AutoCrew and the final cut's SRT is recorded. The founder decides in chat (since 2026-10-07): approving the cut, picking covers, answering asks and confirming publication all count when the founder says it in the conversation. List pending items with `autocrew_review_inbox list{preview_dir:<your working dir>}`, relay each `brief` and file links, and record their verbatim words with `autocrew_review_inbox decide` (founder_words = their exact words; ask back when the reply is ambiguous — never guess). The 「等你拍板」 board still works if they prefer clicking. `autocrew_video handoff / match / confirm / report / register / revoke` and `autocrew_asset add` for covers are closed.

Anything you would otherwise ask in chat mid-edit — rough-cut OK (粗剪), storyboard (分镜), sample (样片), spend (花费), cue sheet (配乐), other (其他) — goes through `autocrew_content ask{content_id, request_id, kind, question, options:[{id,label}] 2–4, attachments:[{path}] inside the project}` so it is recorded; then show the question, options and attachments to the founder in chat right away and wait for their answer. When they answer in chat, record it verbatim with `answer_ask{ask_id, option_id, founder_quote}` (or `autocrew_review_inbox decide answer_ask`) — every kind counts, 分镜 and 花费 included. If the reply maps to no single option, ask back. They may also answer in 「等你拍板」; `autocrew_content summary` (`asks[]`) shows that. Withdraw a stale question with `withdraw_ask`. Mark a cut reviewable with `record kind=cut review=true` (or `mark_ready fact_id=<cut>` for an already recorded file) only when it is really reviewable: BGM placed and the mix checked. Record covers one pair per call: `record kind=cover paths=[<3:4>, <4:3>]` (or the second with `pair_with=<first fact_id>`); single covers without a pair stay incomplete and never show up for picking.

## Jianying source-based rough cut

This section applies only when the user explicitly requests Jianying.

For Jianying rough cuts, import the immutable original recording into the actual native draft and place it on the timeline before cutting. Timeline speech clips must reference the original recording with editable source in/out ranges; users must be able to extend a cut or recover adjacent source speech directly in Jianying. A flattened rough-cut MP4 plus an external EDL/restore map does not meet this requirement. External analysis and renders may support decisions or previews only; never re-import a rough-cut render as the speech master to bypass a native draft/import problem.

Before invoking timeline Smart Rough Cut, verify that original footage is actually on the timeline and appropriately selected. A disabled control on an empty timeline is an unmet prerequisite, not evidence of tool failure. Inspect the proposed deletion scope, preserve a lightweight native snapshot, then apply and review cuts. When native control/import is blocked, preserve evidence and report the exact blocker; do not substitute a flattened master or repeat draft creation. Verify original-source references, adjustable cut handles, native playback, and timeline-matched captions before Gate 1. Existing human edits remain authoritative unless the user explicitly requests re-cutting.

## Continue to the requested review gate

Before commissioning or placing supplementary visuals, keep one time-coded selection map identifying each interval as A-roll, Xiaolu/provider B-roll, explanatory motion, or a deliberate composite. Choose by the spoken meaning: embodied situations, actions and customer interaction suit scene B-roll; relationships, rules and exact wording suit motion. These routes complement each other. Approval of an Opus sample, background or aesthetic applies to that scope; it does not cancel previously planned Xiaolu scenes or approve an all-motion replacement. Reconcile both routes and their actual generation/approval state before extending a sample into a full edit. Storyboard images are not generated videos; pending spend authority must remain explicit.

Place non-overlapping full-frame B-roll and motion clips sequentially on one supplementary video track by default, keeping their original source handles and IDs. Add another track only for intentional simultaneous compositing. A CLI that allocates one track per asset is an implementation limitation to resolve through supported controls, not the desired final layout. Check visibility and overlaps so an opaque motion layer cannot silently hide another approved scene.

After placement, compare every B-roll edge with the A-roll cut points. A sliver of A-roll under about one second — between a B-roll edge and the next A-roll cut, or between two B-roll clips — reads as dropped frames (user, 2026-09-29: “看起来像是抽帧……别让这些穿插过程当中有断裂感”). Use geometry checks to locate timeline slivers; these do not certify rendered continuity. Before every export call `autocrew_content check_slivers{content_id}` (read-only; add `chatcut_project_id` / `timeline_id` if the project isn't recorded yet). Fix each reported sliver by its `suggestion` — hold the previous B-roll over it, or trim the breath gap so the two B-roll clips meet — then call it again; export only at 0, then `record kind=cut`. If it returns `unchecked`, fix the reason (record `chatcut_project`, pass `timeline_id`) or tell the user plainly; never export past it silently. AutoCrew re-runs the same check on the recorded cut and blocks cut approval until every sliver is fixed or the user waives it. Longer A-roll returns are intentional and stay. How to hold in ChatCut: [chatcut-local-editing.md](references/chatcut-local-editing.md). After export, follow [temporal-continuity-qa.md](references/temporal-continuity-qa.md) to check actual rendered frames against the source and native preview. Zero slivers alone is never a dropped-frame pass.

For an authorized full edit, Gate 2 approval starts generation → retrieval/QC → actual timeline placement → transitions/text/grade → BGM/SFX/mix → playable Gate 3 review. Generating files or importing them into the media bin is a progress milestone, not a stopping point. In that context, “导入” means continue the approved edit; an explicit “只导入素材库/只做素材” remains a narrower scope. Do not end a turn with “现在开始插入” without executing it, or wait for another “继续”.

Before showing Gate 3, use [assembly-evidence.md](references/assembly-evidence.md) and the read-only assembly audit. Re-read the actual editor timeline before placement and after any timeout; do not infer insertion from library counts or duplicate a possibly completed edit. If blocked, finish independent authorized work and report the exact operation/error, recovery record and unfinished items. Missing a new flattened A-roll export alone is not a blocker when the current editable timeline and source/SRT mapping can be verified.

## Four reviews

### Review delivery: inline video plus editable ChatCut project

The user gives instructions and watches review videos directly in the conversation; ChatCut Desktop remains the editable project. For every rough cut, motion sample and final-cut delivery in Codex, include the corresponding video inline using native tool media or Markdown media syntax with an absolute local path: `![版本说明](</absolute/path/review.mp4>)`. A plain file link, Finder instruction, side panel or HTML review page is supplementary and cannot replace inline playback. See [assembly-evidence.md](references/assembly-evidence.md) for delivery checks and fallback handling.

Before Gate 1 or Gate 3, verify the correct project and timeline, original-source in/out handles, media availability, duration, playback, caption alignment and editable audio layers. Give the exact ChatCut project name and ID plus the project-owned review files. Do not require a Jianying transfer or native Jianying playback for approval. If ChatCut is blocked, preserve the completed work and report the precise blocker; do not silently switch editors. AutoCrew remains the approval record surface described above.

1. Rough-cut review freezes speech timing.
2. Storyboard-and-generation review approves actual cards, editorial treatment and exact generation/spend scope together.
3. Final-cut review approves the assembled, checked timeline and mix.
4. Cover review approves the matching 3:4 and 4:3 draft pair together.

Accept clear natural approval such as “通过”. Store the exact response and shown version. Never require magic phrases, repeat unchanged consent or ask the user to say “继续”. Partial approval stays partial.

Gate receipts contain per-artifact/shot approvals; a top-level hash identifies the current aggregate receipt, not a reason to invalidate unrelated shots. Update that aggregate from valid child approvals after scoped amendments. Only affected creative or paid scopes return to the matching gate. Semantic-preserving prompt repairs require pre-authorization plus explicit internal comparison, not a new user prompt each time.

## Production defaults

- The first 2 seconds must be real: the creator on camera saying the first line, or a real product/web/app screen recording (creator PiP allowed). No MiniMax H3 scenes, Opus cartoon/flat animation or AI-generated people before second 5 (user, 2026-10-10, after Douyin data showed every AI-cartoon opening stuck in the 500–1100 test pool). Still design the first 5–10 seconds for feed attention on top of that real footage. Read [opening-design.md](references/opening-design.md) during storyboard planning and final opening review; do not substitute a generic body shot or logo intro.

- There is no target B-roll percentage (user, 2026-09-29: “应该根据具体的内容来定，而不是技能包规定死了到底要多少”). Decide shot by shot from the spoken content: does the viewer need a picture to follow this beat, or is it the creator's own experience or judgment, which reads better on their face? Justify each kept and each dropped beat on those grounds. Coverage may be reported as a fact, never used as a reason to add or cut. Keywords/subtitles/tiny PiP do not count as B-roll.
- Every storyboard review output must show coverage percentages (user, 2026-10-20: “给我看分镜审阅输出的要求里要加上分镜头的覆盖百分比”). At the top of the review page, show total runtime and seconds/percentages for full-frame B-roll, explanation-dominant mixed, their union, and remaining A-roll/keyword-only time. Use the current timeline's frame intervals, de-duplicate overlaps, and label planned versus actually placed coverage. Follow [director-quality-bar.md](references/director-quality-bar.md) and [material-review-page.md](references/material-review-page.md). This is a required disclosure, not a coverage target or a reason to reopen unchanged approval.
- Make topic-specific causes and consequences visible. Use jokes only when they clarify the spoken claim.
- Keep media, captions, keywords, graphics, music and SFX independently editable in the active editor; reuse the same ChatCut project and save lightweight timeline snapshots before safe synchronization.
- Before Gate 3, reconcile every planned B-roll shot against the actual placed asset and report its real kind: provider-generated video, deterministic Motion Graphic/video, or editorial still. A storyboard image or slow push is not a generated video, and a planned deterministic animation is incomplete when only its keyframe still is placed unless that fallback was explicitly accepted.
- When real historical examples are available, place them where the narration makes the claim they prove. Do not displace an unrelated generated/deterministic B-roll beat merely because the example is visually stronger, and never borrow a same-named asset from another project without matching provenance.
- Use exact Chinese/logos/numbers in deterministic video overlays. Keep the user's font treatment consistent, avoid splitting short phrases, and check the smallest display view.
- Grade from representative A-roll evidence; no blind LUT/sharpening recipe or promise of recovered missing detail.
- Verify rendered audio and continuous motion, not just timeline settings or still frames.

## Full-media deliverables

- Captions stay a separate ChatCut caption track. Never edit further after captions are burned into picture; any change goes back to the editable timeline.
- After Gate 3 approval, also export a 无字幕母版 from the same timeline: same edit, B-roll and music, caption track off, 1920×1080, filename containing `无字幕`. Register the captioned cut as `record kind=cut` and its SRT as `record kind=srt for_cut=<that cut>`. Register the master as `record kind=clean_master captions_off=true path=<master>` (it binds to the approved cut; AutoCrew checks 1920×1080, an audio track and that the duration matches the cut within 0.5 s, and refuses otherwise). Burned-in captions cannot be detected automatically, so `captions_off=true` is your declaration that the caption track was off: never register a captioned export as the master. The English edition uses it as its picture source and refuses without it. After the cut changes, re-export and re-register the master from the new timeline.
- English edition (lip-sync + English captions) follows AutoCrew `skills/video-session/references/en-edition-recipe.md`. It runs only in the foreground with the ChatCut window open: background Codex tasks (approval policy never) get paid generation auto-denied. Quote the cost before each paid generation and wait for the founder's yes; take and release the ChatCut lock (`mkdir ~/.cache/autocrew-yt/chatcut.lock`) per that recipe.
- The WeChat article cover is derived from the approved video cover: re-lay it out (big text left, person right) per AutoCrew `skills/cover-generator`; do not generate a new concept.
