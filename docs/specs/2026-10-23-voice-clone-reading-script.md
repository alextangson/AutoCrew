# 英文声音克隆朗读稿

**怎么录**
- 用拍 A-roll 的同一支麦，在安静的房间录，别开空调和风扇。
- 用你平时口播的语气，像跟朋友讲事，不要播音腔。念错了停一下，从那句重来就行，后期我来剪。
- 两段一共约 2 分半，不要超过 3 分钟：ChatCut 克隆最多吃 180 秒。
- 录完把文件发我，wav 或 m4a 都行。

---

**Part 1 — explaining (about 70 seconds)**

Here's something most people get wrong about AI. They think the model is the whole story. A new one comes out, everyone runs to compare scores, and they switch. But the model is just the fire on the stove. Whether dinner tastes good depends on the cook.

I work as a forward-deployed engineer. That means I sit inside a company, next to the people doing the real work, and help them actually use AI. Not in a slide deck. In their inbox, their spreadsheets, their customer chats.

And honestly? The biggest gains I've seen didn't come from a smarter model. They came from boring habits. Break the task into steps. Write the rules down in one document. Decide what "done" looks like before you start. And keep your own hand on the send button.

**Part 2 — talking to the viewer (about 70 seconds)**

So let me ask you something. When was the last time you told your AI the same thing twice? Maybe three times? Yeah. Me too.

Last week I tested three tools on the exact same job: turning a messy meeting transcript into a one-page plan. Same prompt, word for word. One of them nailed it on the second try. Another one fixed the number I pointed at, and quietly broke the paragraph I liked. Twenty minutes, three tools, and the answer was obvious.

You don't need a benchmark to do this. You need an old task you already finished, two or three AI tools, and a notebook. Try it this weekend. Then come back and tell me which one surprised you. I read every comment.
